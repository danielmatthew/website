* Back from a week in Zermatt, staying at the lovely [Europe Hotel.](https://www.europe-zermatt.ch) Nice recommendation from [Run the Alps.](https://www.runthealps.com/blog/zermatt-switzerland-guide-for-trailrunners/) Shame I lost my hat though :( Despite coming down with a shitty cold the weekend before, I covered \~90km:
  * [A loop to Schwarzsee](https://www.strava.com/activities/20408255817)
  * Hiking up to [Höhbalmen via the Trift gorge](https://www.strava.com/activities/20421154648)
  * Walking the [5-Seenweg](https://www.strava.com/activities/20435946209)
  * Ran/hiked up to the [3400m Oberrothorn](https://www.strava.com/activities/20449475257) and got weirded out by the intimidating Ibex and isolation
  * Took the [Gornergratbahn to Riffelberg](https://www.strava.com/activities/20460943181) and then ran back to town
* Having a think about what's next for me. Lots of weighty thoughts to consider.
